Samy's Number Names Generator :3

Every number past a thousand gets a name built from Latin roots. Type one in below, or take a look around to see how the naming system works its way up from a thousand all the way to a centillion and beyond.

Accepts a plain number, scientific notation (like 4.2e45), or powers of ten (like 10^33 or 6.8*10^99).

Drawer I — thousand to nonillion

The common ones. 1e3 through 1e30, each with its own irregular Latin root.

Drawer II — decillion to nonagintillion

Everything in between is a combination of one of these with a Drawer I name, like septendecillion (1e54), which is septillion (septen) + decillion.

Drawer III — centillion and beyond

Past 1e303, the system adds a hundreds root. Shown here in steps of one hundred.

Names follow the standard Latin-prefix ("Conway–Wechsler style") convention used for large-number naming beyond millions. Abbreviations shown here follow a consistent scheme of common usage.
